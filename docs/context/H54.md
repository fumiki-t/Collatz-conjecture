# H54 context pack — eventual critical-prefix barrier

**Canonical status:** `OPEN`. The exact statement is the H54 row in
[`../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`](../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md).

## Target and boundary

Prove `M(K_q-1)>H_q` for every sufficiently large `q`, with effective
constants, and independently verify the finite remainder. Through conditional
P54 this would close the stated least-counterexample first-crossing route. It
does not by itself establish P54's hypotheses.

## Required inputs

- P54: conditional reduction `M(K_q-1)<=N<=H_q`.
- E08--E10: exact finite records, certificates, and early failures.
- H20/H57: the unresolved least-positive-residue problem.
- Mandatory adversarial families from `research/registry.json`.

## Known falsifiers and traps

- Early barrier failures `(q,N)=(17,27),(29,27),(41,703)`.
- `A^rB^s` destroys every fixed positive multiplier margin.
- Modulus `2^k` and prefix density alone do not control the ordinary least
  positive representative.
- Larger Phase 1--5 depth is not an asymptotic inequality.

## First useful experiment

State a composable lower-bound or anti-concentration inequality. Test it
exactly on all stored `M(k)` records and small generalized-Ballot layers. Stop
at the least violating word and preserve its affine constant, inverse residue,
carry data, and ordinary height before attempting a larger range.

## Acceptance condition

An explicit monotone lower bound valid for every admissible word, including
carry behavior, with a proof or certificate/verifier pair and an effective
finite remainder. Finite record agreement alone remains `VERIFIED_FINITE`.
