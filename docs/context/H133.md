## Known falsifiers and traps

- `D|B` forces literal finite parity legality but not primitive period. The
  bounded integral rows include powers of the trivial cycle.
- The cycles `(-5,-7)` and
  `(-17,-25,-37,-55,-41,-61,-91)` must remain valid algebraic/resultant
  regressions; a positive-cycle theorem must state where positivity enters.
- A rational affine fixed point is not an integral cycle.
- Profile coordinates are indexed by residues `H_j mod q`, not time `j`.
- The coprime minimum rotation is unique; this fails in general when
  `gcd(L,q)>1`.
- `D_0|D` gives only a weaker noncoprime condition. Do not replace `D` by
  `D_0` inside a claimed full exclusion without proving the loss harmless.
- P158 supersedes the positive-cycle small-area conclusion across all gcd
  classes, but says nothing contradictory about critical area six or large
  noncritical area.
- Defect area may concentrate in a few deep coordinates; P144's triangular
  height and factor bounds do not by themselves suppress the resultant or
  ordinary source.
- EXT15 is a preprint theorem and P139 is conditional. Knight's “high cycle”
  is not every Collatz cycle.
- Resultant divisibility alone does not bound its magnitude below more strongly
  than `D`; an energy proof needs a uniform upper separation.
- Exact resonance is not a near-resonance inverse theorem. P155 remains useful
  arithmetic, but P158 rather than resonance now closes the area-three
  positive-cycle case.
- NG35: `75^7>3*64^7`; EXT05 plus factor separation alone stops at critical
  area five. Do not claim area at least seven from the same scalar comparison.
- NG36: P133's least-value rotation and P156's discrepancy-minimum rotation
  do not coincide for all positive rational shadows. An integer-cycle bridge
  must prove or explicitly transport between them.
- P164/P165 force growth but do not contradict either a tall profile with
  `h_*` of order at least `q^(1/3)` or a diffuse profile whose area is spread
  over many lower defects.
- NG37 prevents treating descent-density improvement as pointwise strict;
  NG38 requires the reduced endpoint term in every weighted polynomial
  estimate. The Phase 28 plateau shows that area can grow while `J,h` remain
  fixed.
