## Known falsifiers and traps

- `D|B` forces literal finite parity legality but not primitive period. The
  bounded integral rows include powers of the trivial cycle.
- The cycles `(-5,-7)` and
  `(-17,-25,-37,-55,-41,-61,-91)` must remain valid algebraic/resultant
  regressions; a positive-cycle theorem must state where positivity enters.
- A rational affine fixed point is not an integral cycle.
- Profile coordinates are indexed by residues `H_j mod q`, not time `j`.
- The coprime minimum rotation is unique; this fails in general when
  `gcd(L,q)>1`.
- `D_0|D` gives only a weaker noncoprime condition. Do not replace `D` by
  `D_0` inside a claimed full exclusion without proving the loss harmless.
- P149 is an all-depth theorem at area at most two, but says nothing about
  coprime area at least three or the noncoprime branch.
- Defect area may concentrate in a few deep coordinates; P144's triangular
  height and factor bounds do not by themselves suppress the resultant or
  ordinary source.
- EXT15 is a preprint theorem and P139 is conditional. Knight's “high cycle”
  is not every Collatz cycle.
- Resultant divisibility alone does not bound its magnitude below more strongly
  than `D`; an energy proof needs a uniform upper separation.

## Acceptance condition

An exact uniform theorem covering arbitrary defect area and all gcd classes,
with an effective finite remainder and independent verifier. It must conclude
either `abs(R)<D`, an incompatible stronger divisibility, or
`C_min<300000D` for every nontrivial positive profile.
