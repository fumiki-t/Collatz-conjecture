# H89 context pack — eventual ancestral-prefix barrier

**Canonical status:** `OPEN`. The exact statement is the H89 row in
[`../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`](../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md). The full derivation is in the
[`Phase 15B audit`](../../research/audits/ancestral-frontier/REPORT.md).

## Target and boundary

Prove `M_star(K_q-1)>H_q` for every sufficiently large `q`, with effective
constants, and independently verify the finite first-crossing remainder. P90
then excludes finite crossing through P54 and never crossing through P89 plus
`H_q>q/6`. The eventual inequality is not proved by E25.

## Required inputs

- P54: finite-first-crossing conditional box.
- P89/P90: ancestral minimality and the repaired two-case implication.
- P91/P92: cross-Q prefix carry and uniform cylinder dominance.
- P93/P94: canonical finite renewal blocks and exact support.
- P95: shifted correction and jump classes.
- E25/E26: finite source/frontier data only.
- NG24--NG27 and every mandatory adversarial family.

P96 is useful distributional context but is not a pointwise input.

## Known falsifiers and traps

- A finite `M_star` record cannot be extrapolated to an eventual inequality.
- Prefixing needs P91's exact carry; endpoint equivalence is not a left
  congruence by NG24.
- Same-Q safe targets are incomplete by NG25, and unsafe targets can yield a
  safe valley suffix by NG26.
- NG27 refutes a universal same-Q compression gain of at most three.
- A positive-measure 3-adic complement need not omit a positive ordinary
  integer.
- Divisibility alone does not imply positivity, literal parity, safety, or a
  smaller source.

## First useful experiment

Define one exact recurrence on a state containing at least
`(Q,L,B,r2,r3,D,carry,source order)`. Before scaling, reconstruct every named
NG24--NG27 witness and the E25 record endpoints. Stop at the first pair that
the proposed state merges despite opposite dominance outcomes; preserve that
pair as a new obstruction.

## Acceptance condition

An all-depth proof of eventual `M_star(K_q-1)>H_q` with an effective threshold,
plus an independently verified finite first-crossing remainder. A larger
source scan, bounded Q frontier, Haar estimate, or empirical compression trend
alone remains finite or distributional evidence.
