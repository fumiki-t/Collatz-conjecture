constants, and independently verify the finite first-crossing remainder. P90

## Known falsifiers and traps

- A finite `M_star` record cannot be extrapolated to an eventual inequality.
- Prefixing needs P91's exact carry; endpoint equivalence is not a left
  congruence by NG24.
- Same-Q safe targets are incomplete by NG25, and unsafe targets can yield a
  safe valley suffix by NG26.
- NG27 refutes a universal same-Q compression gain of at most three.
- NG28 refutes positive same-Q carry at Q=26; signed carry is mandatory even
  though E27 has no negative example through Q=17.
- NG29 caps coefficient-only summed-Haar predecessor pressure below normalized
  count 360.469; unbounded progress requires affine, fixed-source,
  transition, canonical-representative, or carry information.
- A positive-measure 3-adic complement need not omit a positive ordinary
  integer.
- Divisibility alone does not imply positivity, literal parity, safety, or a
  smaller source.
- A bounded repeat rejection rate is not an eventual theorem. Any recurrence
  using P132 must explain repeat-free survivors rather than merely extend Q.

## Phase 25 support input

odd boundaries. Under P54 and pairwise-distinct states, P152 gives

```text

```

The exact q0 interval gives `n_q0=73` and, retaining EXT04, the conditional
lower bound `s>=490186612`. P153 gives the complementary correction upper
bound, but X02 yields only `s<=49708569439`; this is not a contradiction.
Any next support-sensitive H89 rule must use the locations and weights of the
defects, signed carry, or ordinary source. A support count alone has already
reached its current limit.

## Phase 35 full-decoder input

critical-safe image. Repeated least-valuation subtraction uniquely recovers

## Acceptance condition

An all-depth proof of eventual `M_star(K_q-1)>H_q` with an effective threshold,
plus an independently verified finite first-crossing remainder. A larger
source scan, bounded Q frontier, Haar estimate, or empirical compression trend
alone remains finite or distributional evidence; so does E33's bounded
repetition filter.
