# H141 context pack — defect area versus ordinary-source correction

**Canonical status:** `OPEN`.  The exact claim is in
[`../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`](../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md); the derivations and NG32 repair
are in the [`Phase 23 audit`](../../research/audits/defect-area/REPORT.md).

## Target

Turn the exact critical/cycle defect area into a uniform ordinary-source,
carry, or resultant obstruction.  A successful result must help close H89 or
H133; a larger finite area enumeration is not sufficient.

## Required inputs

- P141: exact area/prefix/swap identity and corrected finite factor bound
  `p(n)<=(A+1)(n+1)+1`.
- P142: under P54 and distinct states,
  `K<=A(n_q+2)+2n_q+1`.
- P144/P145: coprime cycle area, triangular height, cyclic complexity, and
  positive-cycle state separation.
- P97/P132 for signed carry and existing H89 repeat rejection.
- P135/P137/P140 for coprime resultants and the weaker noncoprime condition.

## Fastest falsifiers

- NG32: `q=4,c_q=1101100,n=2,A=0` invalidates any proof that silently uses
  the infinite `n+1` Sturmian bound for the finite critical word.
- Concentrated profiles with one deep defect test any convexity or
  "equal-area distribution" shortcut.
- Every E33 survivor tests claims that area automatically improves P132.
- Both Phase 22 negative cycles test hidden positivity assumptions.
- The area-two E34 corpus tests all-area resultant inequalities before scale.

## First useful experiment

Formulate one exact inequality involving the valid defect path, the weights in
the normalized correction, and either signed carry or the ordinary canonical
source.  Optimize that inequality by DP and first search for a profile that
concentrates all permitted defect in the largest-weight coordinates.  Accept
only if the state is prefix-complete and the proof survives NG32 and the E33/
E34 corpora.

## Acceptance boundary

Either prove an eventual H89 consequence with a finite remainder, or prove an
all-area coprime plus noncoprime H133 obstruction.  A bounded rejection count,
contact density, area lower bound, or factor-complexity estimate alone is not
acceptance.

## What this result would not separately establish

Closing only one of H89 or H133 leaves the other P69 branches open.  H112,
H72, H70, and Collatz remain separate. `proves_collatz=false`.
