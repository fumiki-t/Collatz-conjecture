## Target and boundary

Use actual odd-orbit transition congruences, positive ordinary-integrality, or
effective rational-shadow height information to strengthen P72/P75 enough to
contradict every infinite coefficient-safe tail. Under EXT07/P74 the
finite-crossing nonperiodic branch is conditionally absent, so success would
leave nontrivial cycles as the separate counterexample branch.

## Known falsifiers and traps

- The NG21 saturator must be rejected by a genuinely orbit-specific rule; it
  must not be mistaken for a Collatz orbit.
- Excluding one mechanical word does not exclude the safe language.
- Density-one control of `a_i` is not a pointwise lower bound.
- Finite scarcity of transition patterns is not an eventual exclusion.
- Summable `2^-a_j`, divergent `1/h_j`, and `h_j>1` are not jointly
  contradictory; NG22 is an exact countermodel at the formal/2-adic level.
- A 2-adic source is not automatically a positive ordinary integer. Any
  proposed closure must identify exactly where positivity or bounded ordinary
  height enters.
- A summed Haar cylinder mass is not a count of deterministic least positive
  representatives. Ordinary lattice counts contain a per-address `+1`, and
  endpoint 3-adic cylinders may be nested.
- A coalescent endpoint class is stable under a common suffix, not under an
  arbitrary common prefix. `(Q,r3)` alone cannot propagate P81 reductions.
- Finite uniqueness or acyclicity of E23 normal forms is not confluence or
  eventual reducibility of the infinite renewal language.
- The Q=17 surplus frontier is cutoff-sensitive: higher-Q competitors are not
  available for its top layer. Its 32,596 undominated `{1,2}`-gap words are
  finite hard-core evidence, not a proof that an infinite core persists.
- A finite `M_star` record is not an eventual lower bound. P96's positive
  3-adic complement need not omit any positive ordinary integer, and NG27
  forbids assuming same-Q compression gain is uniformly at most three.
- Phase 16's G250/H250 split is not an H72 theorem and cannot be applied to a
  repeated periodic segment. Finite carry positivity is refuted by NG28.
- Phase 17's G270/H270 split is likewise a distinct finite-crossing theorem;
  NG29 forbids treating coefficient-only Haar pressure as the missing
  deterministic positive-source argument.

## First useful experiment

Start from P89/P91/P92's ancestral endpoint frontier in ordinary source,
terminal surplus, and prefix carry, together with P79's coupled recurrences for `(U,V,C_w)` and the
canonical `(r_2,r_3,B)` address data. Seek either a cross-Q carry-aware
recursion proving eventual downward reducibility, or a
separation/large-sieve rule proving one of P80's subexponential ordinary-height
counts while retaining the per-address lattice error. Test first against
NG24--NG29, NG23, both NG22 models, NG21, the `{1,2}` core,
E20/E22/E23/E24/E25/E26/E27, and every mandatory
adversarial family. A range extension is useful only after a candidate
recursion or dominance rule is stated.

## Acceptance condition

An orbit-specific arithmetic theorem that rejects every positive ordinary
integer permanent-safe source, together with a checked finite remainder.
Proving either quantified P80 estimate is sufficient. A smaller exponent
derived only from coprimality is refuted by NG21; a contradiction using only
the four analytic conditions is refuted by NG22; raw Haar substitution is
refuted by NG23; endpoint-only prefix propagation is refuted by NG24.
