# H72 context pack — orbit-specific infinite-tail packing

**Canonical status:** `OPEN`. The exact statement is the H72 row in
[`../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`](../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md).

## Target and boundary

Use actual odd-orbit transition congruences beyond distinctness modulo six to
strengthen P72 enough to contradict every infinite coefficient-safe tail.
Success eliminates only the second branch of P69; cycles and the renewal
ladder remain open.

## Required inputs

- P69: counterexample trichotomy.
- P72: exact normalization and `j^(1/9)` packing envelope.
- P73: exclusion of the single all-contact mechanical word.
- E20: finite convention/adversarial audit.
- NG21: mod-6 packing alone is exponent-sharp in an abstract non-orbit model.

## Known falsifiers and traps

- The NG21 saturator must be rejected by a genuinely orbit-specific rule; it
  must not be mistaken for a Collatz orbit.
- Excluding one mechanical word does not exclude the safe language.
- Density-one control of `a_i` is not a pointwise lower bound.
- Finite scarcity of transition patterns is not an eventual exclusion.

## First useful experiment

Enumerate exact two- or multi-step successor congruence constellations for the
odd map, then ask whether they forbid a positive-density subset of the NG21
saturator. Use E20 and every mandatory adversarial family as convention tests.
Mine the least locally admissible constellation that defeats any proposed
packing improvement before scaling.

## Acceptance condition

An orbit-specific counting or reciprocal-sum bound that contradicts
coefficient safety for every sufficiently long tail, together with a checked
finite remainder. A smaller exponent derived only from coprimality remains
refuted by NG21.
