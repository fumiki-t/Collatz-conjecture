# H72 context pack — orbit-specific infinite-tail packing

**Canonical status:** `OPEN`. The exact statement is the H72 row in
[`../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`](../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md).

## Target and boundary

Use actual odd-orbit transition congruences, positive ordinary-integrality, or
effective rational-shadow height information to strengthen P72/P75 enough to
contradict every infinite coefficient-safe tail. Under EXT07/P74 the
finite-crossing nonperiodic branch is conditionally absent, so success would
leave nontrivial cycles as the separate counterexample branch.

## Required inputs

- P69: counterexample trichotomy.
- P72: exact normalization and `j^(1/9)` packing envelope.
- P73: exclusion of the single all-contact mechanical word.
- E20: finite convention/adversarial audit.
- NG21: mod-6 packing alone is exponent-sharp in an abstract non-orbit model.
- EXT07/P74/P75: conditional reciprocal summability, permanent safety,
  summable octave defects, and the stronger small-defect counting bound.
- P76: exact negative-real companion and moving real/2-adic rational shadows.
- NG22/E21: a formal exponent word satisfies the analytic conditions and has
  a coherent odd 2-adic source; its audited prefix has no positive ordinary
  source below `2^1174`.

## Known falsifiers and traps

- The NG21 saturator must be rejected by a genuinely orbit-specific rule; it
  must not be mistaken for a Collatz orbit.
- Excluding one mechanical word does not exclude the safe language.
- Density-one control of `a_i` is not a pointwise lower bound.
- Finite scarcity of transition patterns is not an eventual exclusion.
- Summable `2^-a_j`, divergent `1/h_j`, and `h_j>1` are not jointly
  contradictory; NG22 is an exact countermodel at the formal/2-adic level.
- A 2-adic source is not automatically a positive ordinary integer. Any
  proposed closure must identify exactly where positivity or bounded ordinary
  height enters.

## First useful experiment

Start from the NG22 exponent word and its canonical inverse-series residues.
Seek an effective lower bound for reduced rational-shadow denominator/height,
or a positivity/ordinary-integrality obstruction that cannot hold for a
general odd 2-adic source. In parallel, test exact successor congruence rules
against both NG21 and NG22. Use E20 and every mandatory adversarial family as
convention tests, and mine the least collision before scaling.

## Acceptance condition

An orbit-specific arithmetic theorem that rejects every positive ordinary
integer permanent-safe source, together with a checked finite remainder. A
smaller exponent derived only from coprimality is refuted by NG21; a
contradiction using only the four analytic conditions is refuted by NG22.
