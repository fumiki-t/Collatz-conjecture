## Target and boundary

Use actual odd-orbit transition congruences, positive ordinary-integrality,
cross-address multiplicity, or effective rational-shadow height information
to contradict every infinite coefficient-safe tail. P222 now removes the
finite-crossing nonperiodic branch internally, so H72 is the sole surviving
nonperiodic obligation; nontrivial cycles remain a separate branch.

Phase 39's P240 conditionally reduces this entire nonperiodic branch to
[`H112`](H112.md), using X02 and P228/E54. P235--P238 supply the carry and
macroscopic jump language for a constructive ancestor route. The conditional
reduction neither proves H112 nor changes H72's status.

## Known falsifiers and traps

- The NG21 saturator must be rejected by a genuinely orbit-specific rule; it
  must not be mistaken for a Collatz orbit.
- Excluding one mechanical word does not exclude the safe language.
- Density-one control of `a_i` is not a pointwise lower bound.
- Finite scarcity of transition patterns is not an eventual exclusion.
- Summable `2^-a_j`, divergent `1/h_j`, and `h_j>1` are not jointly
  contradictory; NG22 is an exact countermodel at the formal/2-adic level.
- A 2-adic source is not automatically a positive ordinary integer. Any
  proposed closure must identify exactly where positivity or bounded ordinary
  height enters.
- A summed Haar cylinder mass is not a count of deterministic least positive
  representatives. Ordinary lattice counts contain a per-address `+1`, and
  endpoint 3-adic cylinders may be nested.
- A coalescent endpoint class is stable under a common suffix, not under an
  arbitrary common prefix. `(Q,r3)` alone cannot propagate P81 reductions.
- Finite uniqueness or acyclicity of E23 normal forms is not confluence or
  eventual reducibility of the infinite renewal language.
- The Q=17 surplus frontier is cutoff-sensitive: higher-Q competitors are not
  available for its top layer. Its 32,596 undominated `{1,2}`-gap words are
  finite hard-core evidence, not a proof that an infinite core persists.
- A finite `M_star` record is not an eventual lower bound. P96's positive
  3-adic complement need not omit any positive ordinary integer, and NG27
  forbids assuming same-Q compression gain is uniformly at most three.
- Phase 16's G250/H250 split is not an H72 theorem and cannot be applied to a
  repeated periodic segment. Finite carry positivity is refuted by NG28.
- Phase 17's G270/H270 split is likewise a distinct finite-crossing theorem;
  NG29 forbids treating coefficient-only Haar pressure as the missing
  deterministic positive-source argument.
- Phase 18 does not make H72 a finite-state problem. A coefficient alphabet or
  finite quotient is insufficient unless transition closure, prefix
  completeness, ordinary positivity, and P111 source-lift stabilization are
  proved. NG30 forbids assuming a one-switch SCC order.
- Infinite mean under P113 does not contradict a deterministic orbit and does
  not permit replacing one canonical representative by an endpoint average.
- A bounded zero-lift suffix does not imply eventual stabilization or eventual
  nonstabilization. Source 167 is the mandatory finite falsifier.
- P116 removes ultimately periodic noncycle stabilization, not positive cycle
  candidates; the cycle branch remains separate from H72.
- Phase 20 finite prefixes cannot certify an infinite word class. Unbounded
  balance and `p(n)-n -> infinity` do not imply positive entropy, randomness,
  or even a linear complexity excess.
- Phase 21 removes subcritical linear complexity but does not imply positive
  entropy. A finite repeat-exclusion rate cannot be promoted to an eventual
  repeat theorem, and P131 points toward large peaks rather than a
  contradiction.
- P220 counts points on one equal-time collision-free orbit. It does not count
  many renewal addresses with the same ordinary height, absorb their lattice
  `+1` errors, or prove either P80 premise.
- P223's improved density-one exponent is not an eventual pointwise defect
  bound. P224/P225 allow an infinite path and do not force P86 descent.
- P231--P233's opposite weighted regimes and different real/2-adic limits are
  compatible. A proposed contradiction must identify the extra positive
  ordinary-integrality, carry, ancestry, or effective-height input it uses.
- P234 is pointwise only on renewal boundaries; do not silently extend it to
  every odd iterate or infer a finite last-small-defect time.

## First useful experiment

Start from P89/P91/P92's ancestral endpoint frontier in ordinary source,
terminal surplus, and prefix carry, together with P79's coupled recurrences,
P231's exact mass, P232's two weighted regimes, P233's term valuations, and

smaller surplus-dominating positive ancestor, or a separation/large-sieve rule
proving one of P80's subexponential ordinary-height counts while retaining the
per-address lattice error. Any use of P220/P228 must explicitly bridge from
one realized orbit to the many-address count. In parallel, add P115's
lift digit and P112's last-valley band to the G270 state and seek a rule that
forces infinitely many nonzero lifts without discarding signed carry or
ordinary height. Test first against
NG24--NG31, NG23, both NG22 models, NG21, source 167, the `{1,2}` core,
E20/E22/E23/E24/E25/E26/E27, and every mandatory
adversarial family. For a Phase 21 successor, require an exact implication
from a repeated/right-special factor, return word, or repeat-avoidance event
to a nonzero P115 lift, signed carry, or ordinary-height upper obstruction;
test it on E32 and the E33 survivors before scaling. A range extension is useful only after a candidate
recursion or dominance rule is stated.

## Acceptance condition

An orbit-specific arithmetic theorem that rejects every positive ordinary
integer permanent-safe source, together with a checked finite remainder.
Proving either quantified P80 estimate is sufficient. A smaller exponent
derived only from coprimality is refuted by NG21; a contradiction using only
the four analytic conditions is refuted by NG22; raw Haar substitution is
refuted by NG23; endpoint-only prefix propagation is refuted by NG24.
