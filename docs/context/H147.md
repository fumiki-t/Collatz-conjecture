# H147 context pack — paired sparse arcs at area three

**Canonical status:** `OPEN`.  The exact claim is the H147 row in
[`../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`](../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md).  P147--P150 and the finite
diagnostic are proved in the
[`Phase 24 audit`](../../research/audits/sparse-arc-resultants/REPORT.md).

## Target

For every valid critical coprime area-three profile, prove an effective
uniform improvement over the generic seven-point circular-gap estimate, or
find a replacement arithmetic obstruction strong enough to exclude that
profile.  This advances only the coprime low-area part of H133.

## Required inputs

- P135: coprime residue-indexed slope root and integrality congruence.
- P147: exact sparse circular-arc divisor, strict size bound, and odd
  coefficient nonvanishing.
- P148: valid area-three profiles have the exact root/edge or
  doubled-root/forced-predecessor shapes.
- P149: coprime positive profiles of area zero, one, and two are excluded.
- P150: fixed-area noncritical profiles are eventually excluded whenever the
  sparse lift is nonzero.
- EXT05: `D>(64/25)^q/2` for `q>12`, used only on the critical line.
- E36: finite shape/arc diagnostics through `q<=100`, not an asymptotic input.

## Fastest falsifiers and traps

- NG33: cardinality alone gives `W/q<=6/7`, but
  `3^6*25^7>64^7`; repeating the generic seven-point argument cannot work.
- The reduced support can have the full paired form
  `{0,s,s+1,t,t+1,u,u+1}`.  Any proposed gap theorem must keep the pair
  constraints, not replace them by seven arbitrary points.
- The finite worst q-arc ratio `35/41` and effective q/L ratio `80/94` do not
  prove a uniform positive margin.
- A q-adic and L-adic arc choice is only useful if its corresponding integer
  lift and size estimate are written explicitly.  A smaller plotted ratio is
  not a certificate.
- Arbitrary-area coefficients may be even, so P147's odd-coefficient
  nonvanishing does not automatically extend from area two.
- Noncoprime profiles do not have P135's bijective residue indexing.

## First useful experiment

Optimize the exact paired support over the two area-three shape families while
retaining the profile recurrence.  Search first for an all-q combinatorial
inequality of the form

\[
\min(W_q/q,W_L/L)\le\log_3(64/25)-\eta
\]

with an explicit positive rational/logarithmic certificate.  Before attempting
a proof, extend only enough to find near-extremal supports and test whether the
finite worst ratios drift upward.  Preserve the first exact obstruction if no
uniform `eta` survives.

## Acceptance boundary

An accepted area-three result needs a proof valid for every critical coprime
profile plus an independently checked finite remainder.  A larger finite scan,
an empirical margin, or a generic support-count estimate is not acceptance.
Even a complete area-three theorem leaves arbitrary area and all noncoprime
cycles open.

## What this result would not separately establish

H147 does not address H89, H70, H72, H112, the noncoprime cycle branch, or the
full Collatz conjecture. `proves_collatz=false`.
