**Canonical status:** `OPEN`.  The exact claim is the H147 row in

## Target

For every valid critical coprime area-three profile, prove a quantitative
near-resonance inverse theorem and a nonzero low-degree resultant obstruction,
or separately exclude each collapsed Type-A/B and near-resonant Type-C
family.  A uniform two-arc gap is no longer a valid target after NG34.  This
advances only the coprime low-area part of H133.

## Required inputs

- P135: coprime residue-indexed slope root and integrality congruence.
- P147: exact sparse circular-arc divisor, strict size bound, and odd
  coefficient nonvanishing.
- P148: valid area-three profiles have the exact root/edge or
  doubled-root/forced-predecessor shapes.
- P149: coprime positive profiles of area zero, one, and two are excluded.
- P150: fixed-area noncritical profiles are eventually excluded whenever the
  sparse lift is nonzero.
- EXT05: `D>(64/25)^q/2` for `q>12`, used only on the critical line.
- E36: finite shape/arc diagnostics through `q<=100`, not an asymptotic input.
- P151: support-sensitive Hamming/factor bounds and exact area-three literal
  Hamming values `2,4,6`.
- P154: exact coprime resonant-grid resultant divisibility, nonvanishing, and
  conjugate-product bound.
- P155: complete exclusion of the exact seven-grid roots `Q,2Q,3Q`.
- E37: exact q=63322 falsifier, rational conjugate intervals, and the finite
  seven-grid remainder.

## Fastest falsifiers and traps

- NG33: cardinality alone gives `W/q<=6/7`, but
  `3^6*25^7>64^7`; repeating the generic seven-point argument cannot work.
- NG34: the stronger assertion that one of the q/L paired arcs always beats
  the EXT05 threshold fails at `q=63322,L=100363`.  The exact widths are
  `54181` and `85875`; direct modular gcd is one, so the row is a falsifier,
  not a cycle.
- The reduced support can have the full paired form
  `{0,s,s+1,t,t+1,u,u+1}`.  Any proposed gap theorem must keep the pair
  constraints, not replace them by seven arbitrary points.
- The finite worst q-arc ratio `35/41`, effective q/L ratio `80/94`, and exact
  seven-grid theorem do not control a neighbourhood of the resonant grid.
- A q-adic and L-adic arc choice is only useful if its corresponding integer
  lift and size estimate are written explicitly.  A smaller plotted ratio is
  not a certificate.
- Arbitrary-area coefficients may be even, so P147's odd-coefficient
  nonvanishing does not automatically extend from area two.
- Noncoprime profiles do not have P135's bijective residue indexing.

## First useful experiment

Split the exact area-three classification first.  Type A collapses to a
three-term relation, Type B to at most five monomials, and Type C retains the
seven paired terms.  For Type C, measure simultaneous q/L arc failure against
best rational grids and search for an inverse statement of the form

\[
\text{two-arc failure}\Longrightarrow
\text{controlled distance to a grid of denominator }d\le d_0.
\]

The conclusion must feed a P154-style resultant whose nonvanishing and
magnitude remain effective under the allowed perturbation.  Rebuild NG34 and
the exact seven-grid `d=7` family before generalizing.  Do not spend compute on
another undirected q-extension.

## Acceptance boundary

An accepted area-three result needs either a proof valid for every critical
coprime profile plus an independently checked finite remainder, or one exact
shape-family theorem with a precise reduction of the remaining shapes.  A
larger finite scan, an empirical margin, an exact isolated grid, or a generic
support-count estimate is not complete H147 acceptance.  Even a complete
area-three theorem leaves arbitrary area and all noncoprime cycles open.

## What this result would not separately establish

H147 does not address H89, H70, H72, H112, the noncoprime cycle branch, or the
full Collatz conjecture. `proves_collatz=false`.
