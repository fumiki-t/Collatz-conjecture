## Target and implication

Prove that every infinite coefficient-safe all-prefix same-Q-geodesic branch
has infinitely many nonzero canonical accelerated source lifts. P115 says
that eventual zero lifts are equivalent to one fixed positive ordinary source.
Finite zero runs and a coherent 2-adic source are insufficient.

P240 now gives a conditional route from this statement to exclusion of the
entire nonperiodic branch. P222 supplies a permanent-safe nonperiodic source;
choose the least among all such positive sources. X02 puts it above `2^49`.
P228/E54 gives an odd reciprocal sum below `3 log 2`, including the initial
source, hence `Y_infinity<2S`. P86 then excludes every shorter safe same-Q
positive ancestor: its source would be below `Y_j/2<S`. This makes every
prefix geodesic. H112 would contradict P115. X02 is external evidence, and
H112 remains open; positive cycles are a separate obligation under H133.
