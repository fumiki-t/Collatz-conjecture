## Target and implication

Prove that every infinite coefficient-safe all-prefix same-Q-geodesic branch
has infinitely many nonzero canonical accelerated source lifts. P115 says
that eventual zero lifts are equivalent to one fixed positive ordinary source.
Finite zero runs and a coherent 2-adic source are insufficient.

P242/P243 now give an internal reduction from this statement to exclusion of
the entire nonperiodic branch. P222 supplies a permanent-safe future;
P221 gives finite normalized height `mathcal Y(x)>x` for every coalescing
positive odd source. Among permanent-safe representatives, finite sublevel
sets give a height minimizer. Any positive literal competitor with larger
coefficient, including an unsafe one after strict-valley extraction, would
have smaller normalized height. Thus every full shortcut prefix is same-Q
geodesic, and P115 gives eventual zero lifts, contrary to H112. The proof
includes the odd/even endpoint and canonical positive-source bridges.

This implication uses neither X02 nor a finite source cutoff. P240 remains
the historical `CONDITIONAL` X02-based route. H112 stays `OPEN`; positive
cycles remain a separate obligation under H133.
